ScrubJay

Uninstall Mac apps cleanly.

ScrubJay removes an app together with everything it left behind — caches, preferences, launch agents, containers. Every removal goes to the Trash, so nothing is ever gone for good.

Download ScrubJay 0.1.6

Free download · macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon & Intel · notarized by Apple · Open source

A Trash, never a shredder

Every file ScrubJay removes lands in the Trash, whether it came from your Library or a system folder. Change your mind anytime.

Careful about whose file is whose

Files are matched by bundle identifier and ranked by confidence. With Chrome and Chrome Beta side by side, each keeps its own files — and anything matched loosely is shown, never selected for you.

Chat history stays put

Messaging apps get extra care: their data folders start unselected, so years of conversations never leave in a bulk sweep.

More than uninstalling

Clear regenerable developer caches — npm, Xcode, Homebrew downloads — and find leftovers of apps you removed long ago, with sizes shown before you decide.
